pyx assistant

a free browser beta from mainline intelligence. local-first — it answers from on-device knowledge and live public data feeds, not a cloud language model.

pyx assistant is the first public surface for marii (mainline artificial realtime instant intelligence). it is extremely early and still being improved. if something misses, that is the beta talking — not a finished product claiming otherwise.

what it is good at

  • voice in and out (online neural speech + optional on-device kokoro)
  • sports scores and standings, weather, math, jokes, riddles, small talk
  • staying snappy because most replies never leave your browser

what it is not

  • not chatgpt, not groq, not a wrapped api chatbot
  • not a full research agent or homework ghostwriter
  • not finished — expect gaps, odd answers, and quiet improvements over time

try a few

  • “what’s the weather in chicago?”
  • “who’s leading the nl central?”
  • “what’s 17 times 24?”
  • “tell me a short joke”
